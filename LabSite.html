About Me:

One of my hobby is playing trading card games, with Yu-Gi-Oh being my favorite and the only one that I am still playing.

What I like about Yu-Gi-Oh:

Comparing to other TCGs Yu-Gi-Oh is more fast paced and with how the game has evolved over the years, players could interact with their opponent disrupting their strategy even when not on their turn which makes the game more enjoyable in my opinion.

How I choose what deck to build:

I do not really enjoy playing meta decks most of the time. When building decks, I usually make the deck with a certain theme even if there is a better option.
